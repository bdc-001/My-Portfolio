### Content Philosophy
1. **Authenticity Over Polish** - Your raw, honest voice is your strength
2. **India-First Lens** - Lean into your unique perspective
3. **Build in Public** - Share works-in-progress, not just finished products
4. **Data + Story** - Combine numbers with narrative
5. **Actionable Always** - Every post should have clear takeaways

### Sustainable Publishing
1. **Batch Write** - Write 2-3 posts in one sitting, publish over weeks
2. **Repurpose Relentlessly** - One blog post = 1 thread + 5 LinkedIn posts + 10 tweets
3. **Document, Don't Create** - Write about what you're already doing
4. **Archive Your Notes** - Turn internal docs into blog posts
5. **Interview Others** - Easier than writing solo, adds perspective

2. **Feature Reader Questions** in posts
3. **Create a Discord/Slack** for readers (when you hit 200 subscribers)
